Questions

What is the Cosmic Femme Moon Planner?

A moon phase planner and lunar journal that runs on your phone. It shows today's moon phase and moon sign, guides new moon intentions and full moon release rituals, lists eclipses and retrogrades, and keeps a private journal on your device.

Do I need my birth date, time or place?

No. The planner follows the moon for everyone, so there is nothing to set up and no birth chart is needed.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android, and Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer. The full planner installs to your Home Screen like an app.

Does it work offline?

The full planner works offline once it is added to your Home Screen. The free demo needs an internet connection.

Is my journal private?

Yes. There are no accounts, no cloud and no tracking. Journal entries are stored only on your own device, and you can download a backup file at any time.

How accurate are the moon times?

New and full moons, moon sign changes, void-of-course times, eclipses and retrogrades are calculated on your device from precise astronomy models and checked against NASA and US Naval Observatory data, to within a minute or two.

Is there a subscription?

No. The full planner is a one-time digital download from Cosmic Femme Studio on Etsy, yours for life, with free updates.

What is the difference between the demo and the full planner?

The free demo shows today's moon in full and lets you try the journal (3 entries), one ritual, the current month and the next 30 days of sky events. The full planner unlocks everything, without limits, and installs and works offline.
